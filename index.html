Kanasu — meaning “dream” in Kannada — is a celebration of the saree and the women who make it part of life. We bring together handloom textures, expressive colours and enduring craft, creating a collection that feels rooted yet beautifully relevant today.

OUR STORY

A saree is more than
six yards of fabric.

Kanasu was born from a simple dream: to honour the artisans behind India's textile traditions and the women who carry those traditions forward.

We look for the details that make a textile memorable — the rhythm of a traditional border, the character of a handblock print, the warmth of a natural fibre and the quiet beauty of a weave made by hand.
